# Ethics And Safety

CantHarm contains harmful and offensive lexical material. The release can include explicit labels related to insults, discrimination, illicit conduct, sexual-obscene content, political sensitivity, and extremist categories.

## Research Boundary

CantHarm is a controlled lexicon-grounded form-sense-audio benchmark. It is not a conversational moderation corpus and not a production safety system.

## Audio Privacy

The release includes 4,823 canonical clips across 11 pseudonymous speaker IDs. Audio is released under CC BY 4.0 with speaker consent.

Users must not use the audio for speaker recognition, biometric modeling, voice cloning, re-identification, or demographic inference.

No speaker demographic distribution claim is made.
